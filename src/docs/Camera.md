# Camera

## Overview

The Camera Tool helps you show your microcontroller hardware setup right inside the IDE. Whether you are a teacher presenting to a class or a maker documenting a project, it makes it easy to stream, inspect, and annotate live footage of your devices.

It is especially handy for educators who want to demo physical circuits live, and for hobbyists who want to record their setup or share a build online.

## How to Use

- **Start Camera** lets you choose where the video comes from. Pick Webcam to use a camera attached to this computer, or Phone to use your phone instead. When you choose Phone, a QR code pops up. Scan it with your phone and it starts streaming, which is handy for a close overhead shot of your board.
- **Cameras** shows up when you are on a webcam and lets you switch between the cameras you have plugged in, so you can find the best angle.
- **Rotate** turns the picture by 0, 90, -90, or 180 degrees to match how things sit on your desk.
- **Flip** mirrors the picture horizontally or vertically.
- **Mark** lets you draw on top of the video, which is great for pointing at a particular pin or wire. **Clear Marks** wipes those drawings away.
- **Pop Up**, in the `≡` menu, opens the camera in its own window so you can drag it onto a second monitor or keep it visible while you work, and **Dock** puts it back.
